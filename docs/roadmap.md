# Research roadmap

Inference Lab tracks 61 open issues organized into five workstreams. Batch 1 establishes the repository bootstrap, pure-Rust contract harnesses for five foundational issues, and pinned default-off related-work gates for three upstream papers/posts.

> [!NOTE]
> Completed Batch 1 items establish deterministic `bounded_contract_harness` verification and pinned `blocked_on_artifacts` / `unavailable_hardware_runtime` gates. Physical GPU, Apple M2 Ultra, Tenstorrent P150, TPU v7x, AWS F2 FPGA, and 27B serving runs remain explicitly open until their physical hosts and upstream artifacts are qualified.
